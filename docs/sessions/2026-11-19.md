# 19 November 2026

Asked: adapt Right-Click Toolbox for REAPER (KallumS/Reaper-Right-Click-Toolbar,
read only, not changed) for Kalibre, so holding Control and right-clicking in
an area brings up the toolbar with the tools. Asked back which areas, since
the piano roll's mouse rules are the user's (0009): **the arrangement and the
piano roll**.

Built (0092):

- `Core/Toolbox.*`: the thirteen tools, their modes, each area's tool; the
  edits the tools make that weren't there (cut into pieces, glue to the next,
  clear a range, Line's shapes as automation points and runs of notes, Time
  Warp keeping unwarped audio where it's heard). `Note::muted`, saved and
  never played.
- `App/ToolBox.*`: the toolbox at the pointer, its own small window (above
  plug-ins on the Mac); icons drawn in the house colours; each tool's pointer.
- `App/ArrangeTools.cpp`, `App/PianoRollTools.cpp`: each tool with the mouse.
  `Controller::applyLive` for drags that show their change as they go.
- Keys 1 to 9 for Select to Play, Cubase's order; the rest in the Keys tab.

Driven in the real app headless (Xvfb, xdotool): Control-right-click over the
lanes opened the toolbox, Draw drew a clip, Split cut it with its line
showing, Control-right-click in the piano roll opened its toolbox (Drumstick,
no Time Warp), and a drag painted hits. A quick drag skipped grid steps
between mouse events; fixed (Erase, Mute and Drumstick follow the whole
path), with a test.

Found: `docs/decisions/README.md` had been cut down to its last line by the
commit that added 0091; put back from the commit before, with 0091 and 0092.

Tests: 191 core (10 new), 113 app (6 new: every tool by mouse, the toolbox, the keys).
Here the app tests need Xvfb without MIT-SHM (`-extension MIT-SHM`): this
container refuses X's shared memory once a window of its own (the toolbox) is
painted. CI's runners don't.

Renders: 28 and 29, the toolbox in each area; 30, a range on two tracks and
muted notes drawn hollow.

Not brought across: Scrub and Jog (Kalibre can't scrub yet), Sizing Applies
Time Stretch, Select Objects Behind, Draw's Line mode (the Line tool does it).
Not yet tried on the Mac: the pointers' look and Control with a trackpad's
