# 15 November 2026: Kalibre from nothing

Asked for: a DAW tailored to the user, on Apple silicon, everything in one
window, with a long list of features (README), following the basic DAW
features document, Apple's HIG and the house colours. Middaw went the wrong
way (generative, Swift); Kalibre starts fresh.

## The route

1. Read the family's repositories: VST-Morpher (JUCE + CLAP, the X/Y
   arithmetic, house look), Miderator and Noterator (JUCE app + Lua, ScaleView's
   `ScaleModel.h`, the chord detector, the test runner), Middaw (Swift; not
   used). The user asked to weigh JUCE/C++, Swift, Rust and Lua: JUCE (0001).
2. The core without JUCE: tempo map with ramps, model, edits and undo, the
   piano roll rules, patterns, harmony, playback lists, modulators, capture,
   notation, files, Lua. 66 tests.
3. The engine: the graph, CLAP hosting, warping, the X/Y morph, recording,
   rendering.
4. The window: transport, arrangement, lower zone (devices, five editors,
   mixer), browser, menus.
5. Tests that render clicks through the real engine, a render tool, CI.

## What looked broken and why

- **Renders were silent.** The graph's output node was added before the
  graph had channels, so every connection to it was refused without a word.
  Fixed by setting the channels first; now in ARCHITECTURE's rules.
- **One-sample clicks vanished** because audio was read at times computed in
  floating seconds. Now read in whole samples.
- **The lower zone shrank to its minimum** for good once the window was small
  at start: `resized` was writing the clamped height back.
- **The transport ran off the edge at 200%**: it now wraps to two rows.

- **The Mac job was cancelled before it started** on `macos-14`: that runner
  image is gone. Now `macos-15`.

Later in the session: ARA hosting (0028), compiled with the ARA SDK on Linux
and on CI's Mac; the mixer's faders and pan as "last touched"; warp markers
drawn and dragged; controller lanes in the piano roll; autosave; freeze.
The first Mac build on `macos-15` passed: AU, VST3, CLAP and ARA compiled,
every test passed on macOS, the `.dmg` was made.
