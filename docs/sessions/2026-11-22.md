# 22 November 2026

Asked: a device like Kalibre Drums, called Drum Pads ("Drums Pads"), of 128
pads, 16 shown at a time, each triggering a sample or a plug-in; samples
dragged over from the browser; choke groups, and volume, pan, mute and solo
for each pad.

- **Drum Pads** (0113), Add > Kalibre > Drum Pads: a pad for each MIDI note
  (pad 36 plays on note 36), named for its General MIDI drum or its note;
  sixteen shown, four by four, the lowest at the bottom left; a map of all
  128 beside them to move through them (click, drag, or scroll).
- Each pad a sample (Sounds tab, Finder; several from the Finder fill the
  pads above) or a plug-in (browser, Plug-in ▾); Volume, Pan, M, S (Unsolo
  All), Choke Group (1-8; the hats in 1 to start; a 2 ms fade, so it doesn't
  click); Rename; Random Samples for the sixteen shown.
- Built new rather than on Kalibre Drums' engine: the audio thread is handed
  the kit through `Swap` (no lock; Kalibre Drums try-locks), pads summed at
  64 bits (0081), with a neutrality test. Kalibre Drums' sample reader is
  shared (`readDrumSound`).
- The drum grid on its track has a row for each pad with a sound; its clips
  open in the drum grid. Agents' add_plugin knows it.
- Found by its test: Random Samples gave the pedal hat (44) the only open-hat
  sound before the open hat (46) chose. Pads of one kind now choose before
  pads that take any of a family.
- Render 43 (`docs/guide/drum-pads.png`); looking at the first, the pads were
  cramped and the map faint: widened, and the map's empty cells made visible.
- Tests: 241 core (5 new), 217 app (8 new: 7 Drum Pads, 1 neutral), all
  passing here.
