# 0020. Notation for reading and entering notes

## Context

Some parts are quicker to write as notes on a staff.

## Decision

One staff per clip, treble or bass by range, spelled for the scale; notes that start together are one chord; long notes are tied at bar lines; rests fill gaps. Choose a value, click a line or space; Option sharpens, Shift flattens. It isn't an engraver.

## Consequences

Grand staff and tuplets on the staff come later.
