Kalibre for Windows
===================

1. Unzip Kalibre-Windows.zip anywhere you like, for example into
   C:\Program Files\Kalibre or your Documents. Keep Kalibre.exe and its
   Scripts folder together.
2. Double-click Kalibre.exe. The first time, Windows may say "Windows
   protected your PC" (Kalibre isn't signed yet): click More info, then
   Run anyway.
3. Choose File > Settings > Plug-in Manager... and click Scan for New
   Plug-ins. Your VST3 and CLAP plug-ins are found once and remembered
   (VST3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, CLAP in
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP).
4. Audio and MIDI Settings are under File > Settings (Ctrl+,), and appear in
   the browser on the left of the window. For the lowest delay, choose your
   interface's Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode) device.

Keys: where the Mac's guide says Command, press Ctrl; Option is Alt; Return
is Enter. Help > Keys... lists every key as Windows writes it.

If Kalibre ever quits unexpectedly, it says so next time; Help > Show Crash
Reports has the report, and File > Open a Backup has your song from up to 15
minutes before.

Scripts: Scripts > Add the Example Scripts puts a few in the Scripts menu.
Your own go in %APPDATA%\Kalibre\Scripts.
