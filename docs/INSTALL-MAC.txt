Kalibre for Apple silicon Macs
==============================

1. Drag Kalibre into Applications.
2. The first time, right-click Kalibre and choose Open, then Open again.
   (Kalibre isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks once.)
3. Choose Kalibre > Plug-in Manager... and click Scan for New Plug-ins. Your
   AU, VST3 and CLAP plug-ins are found once and remembered. (After an
   update that says so, scan once more: Kalibre learns which send MIDI.)
4. Audio and MIDI Settings are under the Kalibre menu (Cmd-,), and appear in
   the browser on the left of the window.

Kalibre uses every performance core for the audio; Use Every Core in the
browser's Audio tab turns that off. If it ever quits unexpectedly, it says
so next time; Help > Show Crash Reports has the report, and File > Open a
Backup has your song from up to 15 minutes before.

Scripts: Scripts > Add the Example Scripts puts a few in the Scripts menu.
Your own go in ~/Library/Application Support/Kalibre/Scripts.
